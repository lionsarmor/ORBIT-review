Original FLAC recordings are retained alongside processed WAVs in source.
Edits: mono mix, low-pass resampling to 12207 Hz, DC removal, peak level,
short fades and trims. See provenance.json for exact edits and SHA-256 hashes.
WAV files are unsigned 8-bit; BUDSFX.BIN converts them to signed VERA PCM.
These are real door recordings, not recordings from AIM or MSN.

Original UI cues: click.wav, send.wav, message.wav, alert.wav
Created for RODDY and provided under CC0-1.0. These are original soft tap and
plucked notification sounds, not classic-messenger recordings. Regenerate with
python3 tools/build-buddy-ui-cues.py. Their levels are set separately from doors.
